# My Favorite Movie: Bottoms (2023)

## About
**Bottoms** is a 2023 American *satirical black comedy* film directed by **Emma Seligman**, who co-wrote it with **Rachel Sennott**. It is an exaggerated comedy that uses humor to tackle feminism, gender roles, and high school culture.

## Why I Like It
**Bottoms** is a story of women's solidarity. Even though the film exaggerates gender inequality, the misogynistic attitudes it pokes fun at really exist, and the characters feel very real.

It is funny, but it also reflects the reality of our world, where men are often treated like kings while women are treated as weak or as objects. I also love that it shows women are far more capable than they think they are.

## My Top 4 Characters
1. **Josie**
2. **Hazel**
3. **Isabel**
4. **PJ**
